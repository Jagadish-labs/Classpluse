HOW IT WORKS

From a faculty tap to every student's screen

Three steps, a few seconds.

Faculty updates the status

Absent, delayed, substitute, cancelled or rescheduled, with a short note. Faculty can only change their own classes.

Every screen updates live

Students see the change instantly, with a bell alert. No WhatsApp scrolling and no refresh.

Students know what to do

Attend, wait, or enjoy a free period, with the next class and room already shown.
